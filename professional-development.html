Professional Development & Lifelong Learning

Students will be able to perform effectively in the library and information professions by demonstrating knowledge of strategies for professional development and lifelong learning. By the conclusion of the master’s degree program, students should be able to:

  1. formulate short- and long-term plans for professional development based on an awareness of the needs and realistic demands of the chosen work setting(s), personal competencies, and the profession

  2. explain the role of the library and information professional in the community, culture, and society

  3. develop a personal philosophy of professional behavior and ethics

  4. demonstrate professional communication skills necessary for interaction, collaboration, and instruction

  5. construct a career plan based on personal and professional goals

  6. engage with a professional network of colleagues via professional associations and communities of learning and practice

Description

For this assignment, I wrote a philosophy statement and professional development plan that reflected on my values, goals, and developing identity as a library professional. In the first part of the assignment, I described the beliefs and ethics that would guide my work, especially in relation to cultural humility, patron-centered service, trauma-informed practice, and the role of libraries in supporting access to information. In the second part, I speculated about my professional future by creating both short- and long-term plans for growth.

Analysis

This assignment, from my first semester as an MLIS student, shows how I was beginning to think seriously about who I wanted to become as a librarian. First, I created both short- and long-term plans for my professional growth by reflecting on the realities of the library settings I was interested in, the skills I already had from public library work, and the areas where I still needed more training and experience. I connected my existing patron service, technology support, and paraprofessional experience to my future goals, while also identifying coursework, certificates, internships, residencies, and professional organizations as ways to keep developing. The assignment also allowed me to articulate a personal philosophy of ethical and professional behavior. I emphasized cultural humility, trauma-informed service, inclusion, critical literacy, and patron-centered practice as values that would guide the way I interact with patrons and understand my responsibilities as an information professional. In addition, the assignment demonstrates professional communication skills because it required me to explain my goals, values, and professional identity in a clear, organized, and reflective way, while also discussing the importance of communication, collaboration, and instruction in library work. While I did also construct a career plan based on my personal and professional goals at the time, my life and goals have changed significantly since I wrote this professional development plan, so I now see this assignment less as a fixed roadmap and more as an important snapshot of my early professional thinking.

Reflection

When I wrote my professional philosophy and personal development plan, I was still trying to understand both the profession and my place in it. I knew I valued equity, cultural humility, patron-centered service, and access to information, but it was harder to translate those values into a clear professional philosophy and a realistic development plan. At the time, I felt a lot of pressure to choose a definite path and describe my future as if it were already settled. Looking back, I can see that I was writing from a place of hope, ambition, and uncertainty all at once. A great deal has changed between when I wrote this assignment and when I wrote the vision statement for this portfolio. My life and goals have shifted significantly, and my understanding of librarianship has become more practical, personal, and lived-in. This assignment shows an earlier version of my professional identity, while my current vision statement reflects a more mature understanding of the kind of librarian I am becoming. I’m proud that my values have remained the same or strengthened since 2022, and that I practice librarianship in a service-oriented, equity-forward manner.
